About Me

I am a Doctoral Researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Helsinki, Finland.

My research focuses on Geospatial AI, Urban Computing, and Spatial Intelligence, with an emphasis on leveraging multimodal data and machine learning to understand urban environments and their socioeconomic dynamics.

My work spans satellite imagery analysis, urban representation learning, and multimodal large language models for urban understanding and spatial reasoning.

I am particularly interested in developing intelligent and scalable approaches to understanding complex spatial and urban systems.

Research Interests

  • Geospatial AI & Urban Computing — Machine learning for understanding cities and geospatial environments.
  • Multimodal Learning & LLMs — Learning from visual, textual, and spatial information.
  • Spatial Intelligence & Reasoning — Understanding spatial relationships and geographic context.
  • Remote Sensing & Urban Analytics — Satellite imagery and urban socioeconomic analysis.

Research Projects

01 / MULTIMODAL AI & SPATIAL INTELLIGENCE

Multimodal Urban Intelligence & Spatial Reasoning

This research investigates multimodal foundation models for understanding urban environments and reasoning about spatial information. It explores multimodal urban intelligence, spatio-temporal reasoning, and the evaluation of vision-language models across urban and three-dimensional environments.

Multimodal LLMs · Spatial Reasoning · Urban Intelligence

Related Publications:

  • UrbanWell: Benchmarking Multimodal Large Language Models for Spatio-Temporal Urban Wellbeing Analytics (KDD 2026) · Code
  • UrbanLLaVA: A Multi-modal Large Language Model for Urban Intelligence (ICCV 2025)
  • SpatialAct: Probing Spatial Reasoning-to-Action Capabilities of VLM Agents in 3D Scenes (arXiv 2026)

02 / GEOSPATIAL MODELING

Geospatial Infrastructure & Urban Mobility Modeling

This research investigates how satellite imagery and geospatial data can be used to reconstruct, analyze, and model urban infrastructure and mobility patterns. It encompasses road network extraction and commuting flow generation, with applications in urban accessibility, socioeconomic development, and spatial dynamics.

Remote Sensing · Road Networks · Urban Mobility

Related Publications:

  • From Pixels to Progress: Generating Road Network from Satellite Imagery for Socioeconomic Insights in Impoverished Areas (IJCAI 2024) · Code
  • Satellites Reveal Mobility: A Commuting Origin-destination Flow Generator for Global Cities (NeurIPS 2025)

03 / URBAN REPRESENTATION LEARNING

Urban Representation Learning & Socioeconomic Analytics

This research explores how satellite imagery, urban geographic information, and machine learning can be used to learn meaningful urban representations and understand socioeconomic characteristics. It investigates representation learning, socioeconomic prediction, and large-scale geospatial datasets for urban analytics and sustainable development.

Representation Learning · Urban Analytics · Sustainable Development

Related Publications:

  • Beyond the First Law of Geography: Learning Representations of Satellite Imagery by Leveraging Point-of-Interests (WWW 2022)
  • Predicting Multi-level Socioeconomic Indicators from Structural Urban Imagery (CIKM 2022)
  • A Satellite Imagery Dataset for Long-Term Sustainable Development in United States Cities (Scientific Data 2023) · Code
